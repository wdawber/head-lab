Join the lab

Work with us.

HEAD Lab takes on a small number of PhD students each year. We also host postdocs and visiting researchers — usually tied to a specific project or funding source. Current openings below.

No advertised openings

We still welcome enquiries.

If your research interests overlap with ours — brain modelling, helmet testing, or exposure monitoring — we'd like to hear from you. Please email a short note (half a page is plenty) describing your background and what you'd want to work on.

m.ghajari@imperial.ac.uk ↗

How to apply

For a PhD application, email Dr Mazdak Ghajari with: a CV, a one-page statement of research interests, and a transcript. Mention any specific projects on this site you'd like to extend.

For postdoc and visiting-researcher enquiries, please include a brief description of the work you'd propose and any existing funding you're bringing.

Funding routes

Scholarships and fellowships

Imperial routes that are commonly used by HEAD Lab applicants. Please get in touch with Mazdak Ghajari in the first instance if you intend to apply through any of these.

Dyson School PhD admissions ↗

The Dyson School of Design Engineering's own PhD page — programme structure, how to apply, and supervisor contact. HEAD Lab sits inside Dyson, so this is the most direct route.

Postgraduate doctoral funding hub ↗

Imperial's central guide to PhD funding routes, deadlines, and eligibility across UK and international applicants.

President's PhD Scholarships ↗

Imperial's flagship fully-funded PhD scheme, open to UK and international applicants across all departments.

All Imperial scholarships (search) ↗

Searchable directory of every scholarship Imperial advertises in the current cycle — filter by level, department, nationality.

China Scholarship Council ↗

Joint route for Chinese applicants to fund a PhD at Imperial through CSC.

Imperial Research Fellowships ↗

Postdoctoral fellowship schemes hosted at Imperial — useful if you're applying for an independent fellowship and want HEAD Lab as a host.

Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP) ↗

Imperial undergraduates can join the lab for a summer research project under UROP.

Looking to collaborate?

We work with industry, clinicians, and other research groups.

I am an industrial partner

We have collaborated with several industries across the world in various capacities — consultancies, Innovate UK projects, and direct funding. Please reach out via email to discuss.

I am a student

We are always looking for students with excellent academic profiles who are passionate about translational biomechanics research. Please reach out to discuss. In your email, explain why you want to study at HEAD Lab.
