HEAD Lab · Imperial College London

Exploring how mechanical loading injures the brain.

Road traffic accidents, sports, falls, diseases, and surgical interventions all subject the brain to mechanical loads that cause injury. We work across computational modelling, impact testing, protective design, sensing, and machine learning to predict and prevent brain injury.
Dr Mazdak Ghajari: Lead for HEAD Lab at the Dyson School of Design Engineering, Imperial College London.

Computation

Brain Modelling

High-fidelity computational models of brain biomechanics, used to predict the effects of mechanical loading on brain tissue.

Sensing

Injury Exposure Monitoring

Technologies for estimating mechanical forces applied to the head and brain in sport, road traffic, and falls — and feeding them into surveillance systems.

Protection

Technology and Equipment Design

Helmet tests that reflect real-world impacts and predict risk of different brain injury types — for cyclists, motorcyclists, industrial workers, and beyond.

Impact

Research that reaches standards, policy, and the field.

EN17950 Headform
We helped to develop a new European standard of headforms, the first of its kind in the world for measuring biofidelic head rotation.
150,000+
Views of our public bicycle helmet safety ratings (hiperhelmets.org).
3 nations
Our e-scooter head-injury research is cited in road-safety policy in Norway, Sweden, and the UK.
World Rugby
Our brain-biomechanics modelling has informed return-to-play guidelines.
