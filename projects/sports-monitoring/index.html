Projects · Exposure monitoring

Monitoring brain biomechanics in sport

Instrumented mouthguards, surrogate brain models, and exposure data from thousands of athletes.

Abstract
Instrumented mouthguards measure translational and rotational head motion in sport. We collaborate with sport governing bodies and mouthguard companies to access thousands of recordings, and develop fast surrogate brain models — built using machine learning — that predict brain loading in a fraction of a second.
Funders
Sports and Wellbeing Analytics, Cellbond Impact Solutions, Royal Academy of Engineering Senior Research Fellowship, MRC TBI-REPORTER
Collaborators
Sport governing bodies, Mouthguard manufacturers

The problem

Brain exposure data from real athletes — across whole matches, seasons, and careers — is what medics, coaches, and sports governing bodies need but rarely get cleanly.

Our approach

Instrumented mouthguards capture translational and rotational head motion at impact. Machine-learning-based surrogate models translate that kinematic data into predicted brain loading almost instantly.

The outcome

A workable pipeline for brain-health surveillance in contact sport, with active collaborations across rugby, boxing, and other contact sports.
