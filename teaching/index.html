Teaching

Mechanics, FEA, and student projects.

HEAD Lab contributes core modules at the Dyson School of Design Engineering, taught by Dr Mazdak Ghajari. Both modules are designed to enhance student engagement through practical projects — the catapult and the water tower — that students consistently rate as a highlight of the year.

Modules

DE1 · MEng year 1

Solid Mechanics I

The fundamentals of mechanics — dynamics, statics, and mechanics of materials — taught with practical examples and demos. The mid-term project Ancient Mechanics: Catapult sees groups of 4-5 use energy balance and projectile motion to predict catapult launch parameters, then test them on the challenge day in medieval-themed costumes.

Lecturer: Dr Mazdak Ghajari

DE2 · MEng year 2

Finite Element Analysis

Truss, beam, and solid elements; failure analysis; buckling; vibrations; non-linear FEA. Flipped-classroom delivery: theory in short pre-class videos, hands-on lectures with discussion. The water-tower challenge has students design and optimise a full-scale lightweight tower, then build it and test it to failure on the day.

Lecturer: Dr Mazdak Ghajari

Student supervision

  • MSc individual projects in impact biomechanics, helmet design, and FE modelling of the brain.
  • MEng final-year group and individual projects on protective devices, wearable instrumentation, and injury-data analysis.
  • PhD supervision in brain modelling, exposure monitoring, and helmet testing (see the Join page for openings).
  • Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP) — please get in touch with Mazdak Ghajari in the first instance.

Student enquiries

MSc, MEng, and UROP students interested in impact biomechanics, FE brain modelling, or helmet testing are welcome to email us with a short note about their interests.
