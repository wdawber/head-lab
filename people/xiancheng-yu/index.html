Dr Xiancheng Yu

Former Research Associate (and former PhD student)

HEAD Lab, Dyson School of Design Engineering, Imperial College London

Xiancheng is a Former Research Associate (and former PhD student) in HEAD Lab. Research interests include Biomechanics of blast traumatic brain injury, New helmet test methods, Computational modelling of high rate phenomena.

Interests

  • Biomechanics of blast traumatic brain injury
  • New helmet test methods
  • Computational modelling of high rate phenomena
